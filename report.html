2   Where should the technician go next week?

S06 — South Hills Fire Station
The logger clock is running 6 hours behind UTC since 24 March (battery swap). This corrupts all six channels for the last 18 days of the record — temperature, humidity, pressure, wind, rain, and GHI are all time-stamped wrongly. No amount of software correction is reliable without someone physically confirming the current clock state, the battery, and the GPS lock.
  • Check first: Is the logger clock still showing local time, or has GPS re-synced it? Read the display before touching anything.
  • Force a GPS re-sync if available, or manually set to UTC.
  • Inspect the battery / solar charge — a flat battery was the root cause.
  • Verify GPS indicator light is active (confirms ongoing clock discipline).
  • Note the exact correction time for retrospective data patching.

Why not S03 instead?

S03 (Valley Library) has a faulty temperature sensor — multiple 85 °C spikes, −40 °C spikes, and an 80-hour freeze — but its other five channels are clean. The pattern (85 °C is the DS18B20 sensor's power-on error code) suggests the probe needs replacement, not the logger. That repair takes more than an hour and requires a spare probe. S06's clock issue affects every channel and can be resolved in a single visit with no parts.

Recommended second visit (future): S03 for temperature probe replacement; S07 for anemometer recalibration (new brand, wind reading ~74 % too low); S04 to inspect the rain gauge mounting for debris.
